## Goal

We currently have a danger zone in the sheet identity tab. It’s alright, but I’d like us to consolidate it a bit.

- Let’s move that section entirely in a settings tab.
- Let’s have 3 actions in there: export (not dangerous), mark as dead (somewhat important), and delete (very dangerous). Let’s use a clean list, with proper color-coding based on action importance.
- As a result, let’s remove the export button from the header of the sheet — it’s not needed all the time and can be in that zone only.
- Maybe that settings tab could be all the way to the right, since it’s a bit different from the others.

## Open questions

- Maybe we could have a save action in there as well just to be comprehensive? Not sure.
- I think we should remove the delete button from the list view, since it’s better to require the user to go into the sheet and into its settings to do that action as to be more explicit and intentional.
