## Context

In the lore of the game, the Protector is a single-member faction. There can always be only one, and when a Protector dies, another Protector gets born. Although they do not keep any material possession, they inherit the memories of the former Protector: their knowledge of the land (the map), and whatever they experienced (their notes). That’s why the game is called “Les Souvenirs du Protecteur” (literally “The Protector’s Memories”).

## Goal

When creating a Protector, we should make it possible to pick another existing Protector to inherit memories from. This would provide the new Protector with the map and notes of the Protector they inherit from.

We should inherit _all_ the notes, not just a subset.
