## Goal

When taking notes, we’ll be inserting a lot of links to various things: to characters, villages, map tiles or more. As a result, it’s important we have a solid concept of backlinks, where we can easily click a link, go to another page, and then see the backlink in the breadcrumbs to go back to where we were.

## Requirements

It is important to tackle the notes (see `notes.md`) and the character sheet rework (see `char_layout.md`) before this one since the tab navigation may influence how we create links to various sections.

Additionally, the block dialog should contain a third option: “Save and navigate”.
