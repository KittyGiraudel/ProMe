ProMe collects the minimum data needed to function. This page explains what is collected, why, and what your rights are.

## What is collected

**Without an account**, your data (characters, maps, journal) stays entirely in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to any server.

**With a Google account** (optional), ProMe collects:

- Your Google email address and display name — used to identify your account
- Your character data (characters, maps, journals) — synced to a cloud database so you can access it from multiple devices

## Why is it collected

Authentication data (email, display name) is used solely to link your cloud data to your account. Character data is stored remotely only because you chose to enable cloud sync.

## How long is it kept

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. To delete it, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/KittyGiraudel/ProMe/issues) and your data will be removed promptly.

## Third-party services

ProMe relies on the following services:

- **Google**: authentication (OAuth). [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.
- **Netlify**: hosting and identity management. [Netlify’s privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) applies.
- **Neon**: database hosting. [Neon’s privacy policy](https://neon.tech/privacy-policy) applies.

No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Your rights

If you are based in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact me on GitHub to exercise these rights.
